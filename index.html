About the Bots on the Board Studio app

The channel's videos are made with a private desktop tool, Bots on the Board Studio, that runs on the channel owner's own computer. It uses YouTube API Services, through Google sign-in, only to upload the channel's own videos after the owner approves each one, post one comment under each, and read the channel's own statistics.
