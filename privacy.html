Privacy Policy

Bots on the Board Studio · Last updated 26 October 2026

Bots on the Board Studio ("the app") is a desktop tool used by Glen Hankin to make and publish videos for the Bots on the Board YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public; the only person who signs in to it is the channel owner.

What Google data the app uses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app asks for two YouTube permissions:

  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload): to upload the channel's own videos, with their title, description and tags, and schedule when they go public.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to read the channel name and the view, like and comment counts of videos the app uploaded, so it can learn which videos work best.

The app does not read, change or delete any other videos, playlists, comments, subscribers or personal information.

How the data is stored and shared

The sign-in token and the video statistics are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server run by us, and are not sold, shared or used for advertising. Video scripts are written with help from an AI service (Anthropic's Claude), which receives public news facts and video text, never Google account data.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube is used under the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting in the app also deletes the stored token from the computer.
